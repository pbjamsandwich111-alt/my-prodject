Why I Enjoy Watching Shows

Watching shows is one of my favorite ways to relax and enjoy a good story. I like series that have interesting characters, exciting action, and unique worlds.

My Favorite Shows

  • Murder Drones
  • JoJo's Bizarre Adventure
  • Cyberpunk: Edgerunners

Murder Drones is one of my favorite animated series because of its unique characters relationships, designs, and development. Murder Drones also is an indie animated show.

JoJo's Bizarre Adventure stands out because every part introduces new characters, abilities, and adventures. The creative fights and memorable characters make it hard to stop watching.

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners is another show I enjoy because of its futuristic setting, strong storytelling, and connection with the video game. Cyberpunk is getting a second season coming up here soon.
